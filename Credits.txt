Bol Processor (https://bp3.tech) is based on initial work by ethnomusicologist Jim Kippen and computer scientist Bernard Bel in the early 1980s.  Their work had been granted support by:

- International Society for Traditional Arts Research (ISTAR, New Delhi)
- Department of Social and Cultural Anthropology, University of Belfast (UK)
- Ford Foundation (USA)
- National Centre for the Performing Arts (NCPA, Bombay)
- Leverhulme Trust (UK)

Further development between 1986 and 1998 was part of research projects under the banners of:

- Groupe Representation et Traitement des Connaissances (GRTC, CNRS, France)
- Association ISTAR France
- Centre de Sciences Humaines (CSH, French Ministry of External Affairs, New Delhi)

Bol Processor has been developed thanks to feedback and cooperation of several musicians and computer scientists.

And to the GMEB folks in Bourges for their 1997 award...

MacOS X and Carbon porting by Anthony Kozar

The interface plays MIDI files with SpessaSynth by Spessasus (https://github.com/spessasus/spessasynth_lib, Apache-2.0 licence), using the GeneralUser GS sound bank by S. Christian Collins (https://www.schristiancollins.com). SpessaSynth also relies on fflate by Arjun Barrett (MIT licence) and stb-vorbis (Apache-2.0 licence). Their licences are in "php/spessasynth".

The default Csound orchestra "0-default.orc" plays piano samples from the Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm (https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html, Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence), and flute samples from the University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples (https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html), as does "Beethoven.orc" with its string samples. Details are in the "ReadMe.txt" files of their folders in "csound_resources".
